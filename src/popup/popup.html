    Apple Calendar + Google Calendar support

    Then, in Google Calendar

    1. Open Settings → Import & export.
    2. Click Select file from your computer and choose the downloaded .ics file from your Downloads.
    3. Pick the destination calendar, then click Import.

    Then, in Apple Calendar

    1. Open the Calendar app.
    2. Choose File → Import… and select the downloaded .ics file from your Downloads.
    3. Pick the calendar to add the events to, then click Import.
