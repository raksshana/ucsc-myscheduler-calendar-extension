UCSC Schedule Exporter

Reading schedule…

Open your class schedule from your UCSC Portal or MyScheduler, then reopen this popup.

UCSC Portal
Portal → Enrollment → Class Schedule.
Then click the extension and export your schedule.

MyScheduler
Portal → Enrollment → Enrollment Shopping Cart → MyScheduler.
Generate your schedule and click View. On the “Potential Schedule” page, open the extension and export.
